        Definitions and data notes
        • Source. Norfolk master dataset: 2026 records request (2017–2025) plus the older WADADAM extract (2005–2016), 597,541 charges. Analysis code: norfolk_analysis.py.
        • Case outcome. Most severe outcome among a case's charges: trial conviction, plea / guilty, CWOF, diversion (pretrial probation, filed without change of plea), acquittal, dismissal (all dismissals and nolle prosequi), moved to Superior (indictment routing), civil.
        • Trial conviction = guilty, bench verdict or jury verdict codes. "Plea" in Norfolk is a container code; the reason code splits it into CWOF, pretrial probation and guilty pleas.
        • Excluded. Cases whose most serious charge is motor vehicle or OUI; open cases (outcomes, timing); placeholder dates (before 1980, after 2027, disposed before filed).
        • Unknown race is kept as its own group. Race and sex are blank for all 2005–2016 cases.
