# Cape and Islands: answers to the 23 Objectives

Only three local Cape workbooks are used. No filing date is supplied; ID-year is an unverified proxy. Missing is separate from Unknown/unmapped/invalid/zero.

## Q1. How many cases were referred to the prosecutor each year, and how has that changed over the study period?

**Not answerable.** Referral counts cannot be established. An unverified case-ID-year filing-extract companion is supplied separately.

Limit: No referral population, screening outcomes, or filing-date field. A case-ID prefix has not been validated as filing year.

## Q2. What is the per-capita case rate (cases per 10,000 residents), so counties of different sizes can be compared?

**Not answerable.** No cases-per-10,000-residents rate is calculated.

Limit: Annual population is absent; annual filing counts are also not verified because filing dates are missing.

## Q3. What share of cases are felonies vs. misdemeanors, and what are the 5 most common offense categories?

**Partial.** Case-level class counts and five representative codes are available in the observed disposed cohort; 9.0% cannot be assigned a definite felony/misdemeanor class under the conservative rule.

Limit: No verified filing cohort, broad offense categories, or within-class seriousness. Missing/unmapped source classes remain explicit; dependent shares are withheld above 20%.

## Q4. What percentage of referred cases does the prosecutor decline to charge?

**Not answerable.** Pre-filing declination rate cannot be calculated.

Limit: No all-referrals table or documented decision to decline before filing. Do not substitute post-filing dismissals/nolle prosequi.

## Q5. How often is the filed charge different from the arrest/referral charge (upgraded, downgraded, unchanged)?

**Not answerable.** No upgrade/downgrade/unchanged rate is reported.

Limit: Original charges, amendment versions, charge sequence identity, and validated seriousness are absent. Matching on code would select unchanged codes by construction.

## Q6. What share of cases are diverted to alternative programs instead of prosecuted?

**Partial.** 1,156 observed closed known-criminal case units have at least one PTP marker. CWOF is reported separately.

Limit: No program eligibility, referral, enrollment, completion, or complete all-charged-case denominator. The literal alternative-program diversion rate is unavailable.

## Q7. Of charged cases, what percentage end in dismissal, plea, trial conviction, or acquittal?

**Partial.** 38,036 observed case units have a provisional outcome; 0.2% are unresolved. Provisional shares retain unresolved cases.

Limit: No complete eventual follow-up or verified filing cohort; case closure, criminal scope and highest-outcome rules remain qualified. PTP is a source marker, not program enrollment.

## Q8. What is the plea rate, and how often do pleas involve a reduced charge?

**Partial.** 6,255 of 38,036 observed case units (16.4%) have a GPL marker.

Limit: No original charges, plea agreements or version history, so reduced-charge frequency is unavailable. No complete all-charged-case denominator.

## Q9. What share of cases go to trial, and what is the conviction rate at trial?

**Partial.** 1,674 observed case units (4.4%) have an explicit trial code. Within these, 37.6% have at least one trial conviction marker.

Limit: This is the explicit-code resolved-case subset, not the share of all charged cases going to trial. Mixed verdicts, extraction selection and incomplete histories limit interpretation.

## Q10. What share of convictions result in incarceration vs. probation vs. fines/other?

**Insufficient data.** 55.3% of 6,430 conviction-marker case units have Missing/incomplete type. This fails the 20% rule; requested sentence-type percentage claims are withheld.

Limit: No complete probation/fine fields; sparse imposed types, filed/suspended labels and unmapped age-limited codes prevent a full comparable sentence taxonomy.

## Q11. What is the median sentence length by offense category?

**Partial.** 1,757 finite custodial conviction-marker case units are assessed; 9.8% lack a usable recorded Actual MAX term. Reportable source-class companion medians are shown where thresholds pass.

Limit: No complete case sentence, concurrent/consecutive structure, validated offense-category map, or unambiguous Imposed MIN/MAX unit assignment. Life is not zero days.

## Q12. What share of defendants are detained pretrial, and how often is cash bail set?

**Partial.** 33,315 case IDs have a first observed dated event; their recorded status profile is tabulated. 22,529 link to the dated closed known-criminal disposition cohort.

Limit: No complete defendant arraignment/custody/payment history, cash-only/surety mode, or verified filing-cohort coverage. The overlap is not coverage of all charged defendants.

## Q13. What is the median time from filing to disposition, and does it differ by case type?

**Not answerable.** Median filing-to-disposition time cannot be calculated.

Limit: Filing/initiation date is absent from both charge extracts. Offense date and case-ID year are not filing dates. Offense-to-disposition is shown only as a separate Q19 companion.

## Q14. Do declination, dismissal, and incarceration rates differ by defendant race/ethnicity?

**Partial.** Reportable dismissal/nolle comparisons and White reference ratios are supplied. Incarceration shares are withheld for groups failing sentence coverage; declination is unavailable.

Limit: No referral screening, separate ethnicity, population exposure, prior record or case facts. Source-class incompleteness selects the cohort; these are descriptive associations.

## Q15. Do those outcomes differ by sex and age group?

**Partial.** Recorded-sex comparisons with Male reference ratios are available for reportable cells; age-group results are unavailable.

Limit: No age/date of birth, declination, complete sentence history, or complete gender identity measure. Q14 coverage and cohort-selection limitations apply.

## Q16. Do racial disparities remain when you compare within the same offense category and prior-record level?

**Not answerable.** The requested offense-by-prior-record controlled comparison cannot be performed.

Limit: No complete prior record, uniform lookback, or validated offense-category/seriousness crosswalk. Same-class comparisons alone do not answer this question.

## Q17. Did any of the above metrics shift noticeably around a policy change, new DA term, or 2020?

**Partial.** Observed closed known-criminal disposition case counts changed -42.2% from 2019 to 2020. Descriptive disposition-year trends are shown.

Limit: No verified filing dates, policy/DA-term timeline or counterfactual. Differences can reflect backlog, case mix, extraction and recording changes.

## Q18. How does this county rank against the state average and the other counties on the team’s core metrics (rates from Q4, Q7, Q10, Q14)?

**Not answerable.** No state average or county ranking is calculated.

Limit: Comparable other-county/statewide data and harmonized units/outcomes are absent; Cape lacks filing dates and complete sentencing fields.

## Q19. How does the number of recorded charges per case relate to the time from offense to disposition?

**Partial.** 34,894 linked case units have usable offense-to-disposition intervals; medians/IQRs by recorded charge burden are supplied.

Limit: No verified filing cohort, stable charge ordinal history, complete case facts or unresolved-case follow-up. It is not a causal effect or forecast.

## Q20. Do dismissal, conviction, and diversion rates differ across courts when comparing cases of the same offense severity?

**Partial.** Same-definite-source-class dismissal/nolle, conviction-marker and recorded-PTP comparisons are supplied for reportable court cells.

Limit: No validated within-class seriousness, program participation or prior record. Court labels/assignment and missing severity limit comparability; PTP is not a full diversion measure.

## Q21. Which agencies account for the largest share of filed charges, and how does their offense mix differ?

**Partial.** In the full clean filing extract, Barnstable Police Department supplies 21.3% of charge rows. Class/code mix is tabulated.

Limit: No verified filing dates, so a common 2017–2024 filed-charge denominator cannot be applied. No broad offense-category crosswalk or complete referrals.

## Q22. When both amounts are recorded, how often is bail set lower than, equal to, or higher than the amount requested?

**Answerable.** Among 8,421 paired events: lower 55.7%, equal 39.0%, higher 5.3%. Pairs are 23.0% of dated bail events.

Limit: Conditional observed-event answer only; repeated events can weight some cases more. One-paired-event-per-case sensitivity is provided; no actual payment information.

## Q23. How complete are the race, offense severity, judge, and bail-amount fields, and how does completeness differ across counties?

**Partial.** Cape field completeness, date quality, case-severity incompleteness, sentencing gaps, Missing/Unknown/unmapped codes, and zero-versus-missing amounts are supplied.

Limit: No other-county numerical inputs, so no cross-county completeness difference is reported. Case-level and source-row denominators differ and must be matched before comparison.
